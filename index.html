Process

How it works

A simple monthly rhythm. You know what is being shot, when it lands, and what it costs before the month starts.

1

Batched shoot days

We come to your business on scheduled shoot days and film a month of content in focused sessions, so shoot time is spent shooting.

2

Edit and caption

Every video is edited end to end with captions written in your brand's voice, then delivered to you ready to post.

3

Posted and managed

With the management add-on, we post the videos on the package rhythm and run the pages day to day. Either way, the month is planned in advance and videos do not roll over.
